About me

Hi, I'm Angela Hoang. I'm a private Vietnamese tutor and I run ENGine. I've been tutoring for 3 years.

I grew up speaking Vietnamese at home, and I teach it because I love seeing people use it in real life, whether that be talking with their grandparents, ordering food, or travelling to Vietnam.

If you are unsure about how to get started, we will talk about why you want to learn, and I will check your level with a short chat. Then we will practise a few useful phrases, so you'll leave your first lesson already able to say something.

To get started, send me a message. Tell me who the lessons are for and which level sounds right. I'm happy to share my rates and book your first lesson.
